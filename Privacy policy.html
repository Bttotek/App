Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

Welcome to Civil Engine BBS Calc (“the App”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App and its related web application.

Privacy at a glance: The App is designed to process engineering calculations and project information locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your calculation sheets or project data as part of the App's core functionality.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide personal information for its core calculation features.

Calculation inputs, saved measurements, project sheets and related tool data may be stored locally on your device using browser/app local storage. This information is used to provide features such as saving, restoring and working with your calculations.

2. Data Transmission

The core calculation data described above is not intentionally uploaded to a BTTOTEK server by the App. If you use an operating-system or third-party feature such as sharing, exporting, opening a website, or sending a file, the information you choose to share may be handled by that selected service according to its own privacy policy.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

The App may use local storage or similar browser storage technologies to save preferences, measurements, calculation history and project data. These are stored on your device and can normally be removed by clearing the App's data or the relevant browser storage.

4. Third-Party Services

The current core web application does not intentionally use Google AdMob, Google Analytics, OneSignal or other advertising/analytics SDKs to collect your calculation data.

The Android version may use standard Android/Google platform components required for app operation, distribution, security or stability. Such platform services may process limited technical information under their own policies.

5. Sharing and Export Features

The App may provide share, copy, PDF, Excel or other export functions. These functions are user-initiated. When you choose to share or export information, the selected content is passed to the destination you choose, such as another app or a file location.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures within the design of the App to keep calculation and project information on the user's device. However, no electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should maintain your own backup of important project information.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general engineering utility and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the App.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, services or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy practices, please contact the developer through the official BTTOTEK Solutions communication channel or the contact method provided with the app listing.

10. Engineering Disclaimer

Civil Engine BBS Calc is a calculation and estimation utility. Calculation results should be reviewed by a qualified engineer or competent professional before being used for construction, fabrication, structural or other safety-critical decisions.
