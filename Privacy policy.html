Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to Civil Construction Calculator (“the App”), developed by BttoTek Solutions. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application and related services.

Privacy at a glance: The App is designed offline-first. Your calculation sheets, measurements, BBS tables, and local project data are processed and stored locally on your device using SQLite/Room database. We do not harvest or store your personal engineering files on our servers.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require user registration or personal account sign-in for basic usage.

All calculation inputs, Bar Bending Schedules, site measurements, material estimates, and notes remain strictly on your device storage.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support the free version and premium features of the App, we integrate verified third-party SDKs:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to deliver banner and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, and general diagnostic information to serve ads and prevent fraud according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: In-app subscriptions (such as Ad-Free Premium Access) are securely handled directly by Google Play Billing. BttoTek Solutions never collects, receives, or stores your credit card, bank details, or financial credentials.
  • Firebase Services: May be used strictly for stability, app performance diagnostics, or crash monitoring.

3. Local Storage

The App uses Android Room SQLite database and DataStore preferences to save your work history, recent measurements, rebar configurations, and UI theme preferences. You can completely wipe this data anytime by clearing the App's data in Android system settings.

4. Export and Document Sharing

The App includes features to export Bar Bending Schedules and measurement books to PDF, Excel (.xls), or copy text for messaging. All document files are compiled locally using your device's native components. The file is only transmitted or shared when you explicitly initiate the Android system share sheet.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard best practices to ensure all calculations and stored projects stay confined to the app's sandboxed internal storage. However, users are encouraged to maintain export backups of critical site estimates.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is a professional civil engineering calculation utility and is not aimed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory compliance. Any updates will be published directly on this page.
