❓ Help & FAQ

How to create a Bar Bending Schedule?

1. Go to 1. PROJECT INIT tab and enter your project name & section heading.

2. Switch to 2. INPUT BAR DATA tab, select bar diameter, clear span/spacing, and hit INSERT BAR.

3. Go to 3. EXCEL GRID tab to view diameter-wise breakdown and export Excel.

How to export report to Excel?

Navigate to the grid tab and click DOWNLOAD EXCEL. Clean A4 landscape formatted spreadsheet documents will download instantly.
